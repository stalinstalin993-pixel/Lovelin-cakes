The baker

The person behind the piping bag.

Hi, I'm Ananya. LC Homemade started with a borrowed oven, a stubborn pistachio cake, and a habit of baking for every small celebration in the family. Today it's a home kitchen in R.S. Puram, still doing things the slow way: real butter, fruit in season, and buttercream mixed in small batches.

I don't keep a wall of cake photos to copy. Tell me who the cake is for, what they love, and the kind of celebration you're planning. We'll design something that feels like them — even if the reference is just a colour and a feeling.

Ask a question
How far in advance should I order?

Order 1–2 days in advance for most cakes. Custom and tiered designs need 3–5 days. Selected designs are available same-day; message us on WhatsApp to check what's possible today.

Do you deliver, and what does it cost?

Delivery is free within 5 km of R.S. Puram, Coimbatore. Beyond 5 km, delivery is ₹50. Pickup from our home kitchen is also available during working hours.

Can every cake be made eggless?

Most cakes have an eggless option for ₹100 per kg. A few recipes — tiramisu, hazelnut opera and Basque cheesecake — contain egg and are marked on the cake card. If you're ordering custom, tell us when you enquire.

How do I pay?

UPI or cash on pickup/delivery. Custom and tiered designs need a 50% advance to block the date. You'll get a payment confirmation on WhatsApp.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup or delivery. Within 24 hours, the advance is non-refundable because ingredients and design time are already committed.

Can I send a reference photo?

Yes — please do. Send it on WhatsApp with your date, guest count and budget. We'll tell you what translates well and suggest the closest design we can make beautifully.
